FinTrack Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Overview

FinTrack Pro is a personal finance management web application designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. All transaction parsing, calculations, and data retention occur entirely on the client side in your personal web browser.

2. Google User Data & Gmail API Compliance

When you connect your Gmail account via Google OAuth 2.0 (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly):

  • Strict Local Processing: Emails from your financial institution (HDFC Bank) are read directly inside your browser. No email bodies, subjects, headers, or messages are ever transmitted to any third-party server, cloud database, or external backend.
  • Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, broker, or monetize your financial or personal data.
  • Zero AI Model Training: Your email content is never used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Limited Use Requirement: FinTrack Pro's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Local Data Storage & Control

Transactions and user configurations are stored solely in your web browser's localStorage. You retain 100% control over your data and can completely erase it at any time using the "Clear All" button or by clearing your browser cache.
